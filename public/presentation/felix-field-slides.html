One connected platform turns field data into early, prioritised action for farmers

1

Capture: connected field data

Sensors track soil moisture, pH/acidity and chemicals; drones add imagery; field officer upload photos.

2

Elaborate / Monitor: one central dashboard

Proprietary algorithms fuse photos, soil data and weather to flag, per field: disease, water or fertiliser needs, harvest readiness.

3

Decide: GenAI-powered planning

GenAI proposes the best response to each issue and ranks them by priority, so the officer knows where to act first.

4

Alert: farmers notified on the spot

Platform-linked colour-coded lamp posts signal field status; standard messages also reach farmers.
